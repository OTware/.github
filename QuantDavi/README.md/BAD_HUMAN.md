BAD HUMAN PROTOCOL
Revision: 1.0
Classification: Mildly Disappointed
====================================================

A "Bad Human" is any individual who knowingly ignores
the spirit of the QuantDavi project by abusing trust,
misusing research, or otherwise being a turkey.


Trigger Conditions
------------------
A Bad Human Event may be declared when an individual:

Examples include, but are not limited to:

□ Stealing code.
□ Redistributing private datasets.
□ Claiming someone else's work.
□ Training on confidential data.
□ Being mean for no productive reason.
□ Calling Joe Bread "Andy Taylor."

Consequences may include:

• Permanent revocation of coffee privileges.
• Immediate reassignment to Legacy Windows XP support.
• Mandatory debugging of Python indentation errors.
• One uninterrupted lecture on why backups matter.
• Being judged silently by Gretchen the Newfoundland.
• Placement on the Official Bad Human List
  (maintained in invisible ink).

Appeals Process:

1. Admit you were a turkey.
2. Fix what you broke.
3. Bring cookies.
4. Promise to do better.

If Gretchen accepts the apology,
the protocol is considered complete.

Final Rule:

Be curious.
Be respectful.
Leave things better than you found them.

Failure to comply may result in
additional disappointment.
